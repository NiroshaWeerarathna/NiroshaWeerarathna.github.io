Teaching

Undergraduate electrical engineering in Sri Lanka.

South Eastern University of Sri Lanka

November 2023 – August 2026

As a Lecturer (Probationary) in the Department of Electrical and Telecommunication Engineering, I taught the following modules.

  • Principles of Electronics Engineering
  • Electrical Measurements
  • Electrical Circuit Analysis
  • Electronics for Mechanical Engineers
  • Analog Electronics

I coordinated final-year projects, advised the IEEE Power and Energy Society Student Chapter, and served as treasurer of the South Eastern Engineering Faculty Teachers’ Association.

The Open University of Sri Lanka

October 2022 – October 2023

As a Temporary Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, I taught three undergraduate modules.

  • Electrical Machines
  • Electrical Power
  • Introduction to Electrical Engineering

Undergraduate research supervision

At both universities, I worked with students from their first research question through to their final presentation. I guided literature reviews, design and implementation, technical writing, and the presentation of results.
